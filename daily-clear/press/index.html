Daily Clear

Press kit. A daily block puzzle you solve rather than survive, by a solo developer in India.

Get it on Google Play

What it is

Daily Clear is a block puzzle you solve, not one you survive. Each day there's one board and a small set of pieces, all on show from the start. Place every piece so that rows and columns clear and nothing is left on the board.

What's different

  • Every puzzle can be solved. Each one is built backwards from an empty board, so a solution always exists.
  • Every piece is shown from the start: no surprise pieces, and nothing is dealt to make you lose.
  • The same daily puzzle for everyone. You can share your result as text with a picture of the starting board, and it never gives the solution away.
  • Difficulty rises through the week: gentle on Monday and Tuesday, thinking a few pieces ahead by the weekend.
  • No timer and no lives. Undo as often as you like. Solving with no undos earns a Perfect.
  • Endless practice puzzles at Easy, Medium and Hard, which never touch your streak.
  • Works offline, with no account. Progress stays on the device.
