Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 October 2026

Who we are

Sanoj Technologies is a career and personality development practice that provides coaching, resume optimization, and interview preparation for technology professionals. This policy explains what information we collect when you use this website or contact us, and what we do with it.

Information you give us

If you contact us by phone, email, or WhatsApp, we receive whatever you choose to share - for example your name, contact details, and details about your career goals. We use it only to respond to your enquiry and to provide the services you ask for.

Our WhatsApp links open WhatsApp, a service operated by Meta. Anything you send through WhatsApp is also governed by WhatsApp’s own privacy policy.

Website analytics

We use Cloudflare Web Analytics to understand how many people visit this site and how quickly it loads. It is designed to be privacy-friendly:

  • It does not use cookies or any other client-side state to track you.
  • It does not fingerprint you using your IP address or browser details.
  • It records aggregate information such as page views, the referring site, approximate location (country or city level), browser and device type, and page performance timings.

Because this analytics is cookieless and does not identify you, it does not require a consent banner. Cloudflare retains this data for up to six months.

Hosting

This website is served by GitHub Pages. Like most hosting providers, GitHub may automatically record standard technical logs such as your IP address for security and abuse prevention. We do not have access to individual log entries.

Our Android apps

We also publish two Android apps. They handle data differently from this website and each has its own privacy policy:

  • Offline PDF & Photo Compressor - compresses photos and PDFs on your device.
  • Minutely: AI Notes & MOM Maker - transcribes and summarises meetings on your device.

Both apps process your files and audio on your phone and do not upload them. Both are free and supported by advertising, which is described in each app’s own policy. This page covers only this website.

What we do not do

  • We do not run advertising or advertising cookies on this website. (Our Android apps do show ads; see their own policies above.)
  • We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information.
  • We do not build advertising profiles of visitors to this site.

How long we keep information

We keep enquiry messages and the details you share with us for as long as needed to respond and to maintain our working relationship, or as long as the law requires. You can ask us to delete them at any time.

Your rights

We aim to handle personal data in line with applicable Indian law, including the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023. Subject to that law, you may ask us to:

  • confirm what personal data of yours we hold, and give you a copy;
  • correct anything that is inaccurate;
  • erase data you no longer want us to keep;
  • withdraw any consent you previously gave.

To make a request, email contact@sanojtechnologies.com or call +91-9840916039. If you are not satisfied with our response, you may raise the matter with the Data Protection Board of India.

Children

This website and our services are intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

Changes to this policy

If we change how we handle personal data, we will update this page and revise the date at the top.
