Minutely: AI Notes & MOM Maker

Press kit. Meeting notes written on your phone: record, transcribe and summarise with nothing uploaded.

Get it on Google Play Trailer

What it is

Tap Record when the meeting starts and Stop when it ends. Minutely writes the full transcript, tells the speakers apart and writes the notes on your phone, in the background, even after you close the app. A notification tells you when they're ready.

Minutely needs Android 10 or later and a phone with at least 6 GB of memory. It works in English only. Record only with everyone's consent.

What's different

  • Recording, transcript and notes stay on the phone. No account, no sign-up, no upload.
  • Works offline, even in flight mode.
  • Speaker labels: name each voice once in "Who was speaking?" and the notes use the names.
  • Three notes styles: Executive summary, Quick standup and Action checklist, each with key decisions and action items with owners.
  • Works in the background: notes are written after you stop, even if the app is closed. Phone calls pause the recording, and it resumes afterwards.
  • Live captions while you record with the screen on.
  • Teach it names and terms so they're spelled right, then search every transcript and note.
